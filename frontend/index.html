Privacy boundaries & model capabilities

Local-first financial paperwork assistance. Built for transparency, privacy, and deterministic control.

Local privacy boundary

By default, files and database records stay entirely on your local machine (bound to 127.0.0.1). Documents never leave your device unless you explicitly export CSV data or configure an external integration.

What the AI model can & cannot do

Local open-weight models propose structured fields from messy receipts. However, smaller models can hallucinate numbers. The model proposes; deterministic arithmetic guards and human review verify before anything counts.

Not an accounting replacement

LedgerLeaf is a review and extraction assistant. It does not replace a professional certified accountant, tax adviser, or double-entry accounting software.
